I want you to redesign the ICLAX DIGITAL website to reflect a modern, professional Nigerian digital marketing agency.

IMPORTANT: I do not want a generic American or European-looking digital agency website.

I want a website that Nigerian entrepreneurs, business owners, startups and companies can immediately relate to and trust.

The website should have a modern Nigerian corporate identity, inspired by successful Nigerian technology companies, fintech brands and professional business websites.

1. BRAND COLOURS

Use a Nigerian-inspired colour palette:

Primary green: #008751
Deep green: #006B40
White: #FFFFFF
Navy blue: #102A43
Gold accent: #D9A441
Light background: #F4F7F5

Use green as the dominant brand colour.

Use white for clean backgrounds, navy blue for professional sections and gold for subtle highlights.

Do not make the entire website dark.

The overall appearance should be bright, welcoming, energetic and professional.

	•	Reliability.
	•	Local understanding.
	•	International-quality digital services.

Make Nigerian business owners feel that this agency understands their challenges and opportunities.

EXPLORE OUR SERVICES
